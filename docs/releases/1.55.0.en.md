- **Elevation, up and down.** Your outing now shows the descent next to the climb, and both are saved to Health.
- **Live everywhere.** During a workout, follow your climb and descent on the watch, on the iPhone and in the Live Activity on the Lock Screen.
- **Two outings that were really one.** From an outing's details, merge it with the one before or after, and split them again if you change your mind. Health is left untouched.
- **A workout that outlives the app.** If the watch app restarts in the middle of a workout, it picks up the same outing instead of starting a second one.
