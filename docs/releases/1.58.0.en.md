- **Foulée answers Siri.** “Start an outing with Foulée” starts your walk or run, on your iPhone or your watch. “Where am I at in Foulée” gives you your streak, your steps and your minutes without opening the app.
- **In Control Center and on the Action button.** “Start an outing” and “I had a glass” start with a single gesture, from the iPhone or the Apple Watch Ultra.
- **Your outing safe from a flat battery.** If your watch drops below 5%, Foulée ends the outing and saves it to Health before it shuts down.
- **When something is missing, Foulée tells you.** Health, location or motion denied, weather gone quiet: a clear message and the button to fix it.
- **Report a problem, without sending anything behind your back.** In Settings › About, attach a summary of the app and its latest incidents to your report. Nothing leaves without you.
