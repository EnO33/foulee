After each outing, a notification tells you what stood out: your longest walk in 6 months, a faster pace than usual, more climb than usual, or how steady your week has been. Tap it to open the outing in your recap.

Elevation now shows up everywhere: in the weekly and monthly recap, on each outing, on the image you share, and the Apple Watch now measures it during your sessions.

Turn the notification on or off in Settings › Notifications › “After a session”.
